    About the numbers

    Date filters apply to the creation dates of questions, answers, articles, and comments. Post score is the current net score of posts created in that period. API v3 does not expose separate upvote and downvote totals for these posts.

    Historical net reputation change and account dates/status require the Enterprise v3 management endpoints and sufficient permission. If unavailable, their CSV cells are blank. Current reputation is always a separate column.

    Direct and group subject matter expert assignments are included. The report contains only content the token can read; deleted or inaccessible content may be absent. A local HTML page also requires the API to allow browser requests from its origin.
